SOURCE / PROVENANCE / SCOPE

A spatial atlas, not a copied interface.

Human Atlas — Body Cartography is an independently designed anatomy-learning interface. Its interaction language is built around spatial mapping: Survey, Dissect, Ghost, Lineage and Pathway.

The geometry package is the official BodyParts3D 4.0 adult male reference anatomy. Every imported source mesh remains individually addressable, while on-screen labels are decluttered dynamically instead of showing 2,234 names at once.

This experience is educational and is not a diagnostic or clinical decision-support tool.

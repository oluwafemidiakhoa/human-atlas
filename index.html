SOURCE & SCOPE

Built for independent learning

This branch is an interaction prototype. Its simple geometry is intentionally not presented as the final anatomical dataset.

The production viewer is designed to ingest BodyParts3D geometry and identifiers with explicit source attribution and license metadata.

Human Atlas is independently designed around five verbs: Explore, Reveal, X-Ray, Relate, Trace. It does not reproduce the layout, wording, or signature interface of a third-party anatomy application.
